# US-008 Organize detachments and commanders

**As a** player with a finished roster  
**I want to** assign units to detachment card slots and mark commanders  
**So that** the list matches how I will play the army on the table

## Acceptance criteria

- Organize Army lets me assign each roster entry to one of six detachment slots, or leave it unassigned.
- I can mark one commander per slot.
- Organized lists show detachment and commander badges in the builder and in My Armies.
- Organization is stored with the saved army and included in export.
