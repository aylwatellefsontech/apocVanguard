# Vanguard 40k.

Units in Apocalypse vanguard have the following general design.

## General Design
The core of the game attempts to be straightforward, an easy to play, with less looking up rules besides base stats.  The strategy should come from the core rules, and the complexity from the combinations of different armies, scenarios, and the strategic asset cards.

## Abilities
Units should in general not be overloaded with special rules, weapons and the like. Base units should try to stick to the keywords and common abilities, and generally not exceed 2-3.  Special Abilities (ones with rules in the profile) should be used more sparingly, and units should in general not have more than 1-2.

Together, the abilties should not go over 3-5.

On occasion, particularly special units might have more, but they should be an extreme rarity - try to use core states and common abilities to model units.

## Units

Units should attempt to balance around the Imperial Guard Squad, and the Space Marine Tactical Squad, which model to model should generally have a value of 1:5 or 1:6.

```

Weapons Melee Melee User 8+/10+ | Small Arms 24" User 8+/10+
```

## Characters

### Light Characters
The majority of Light Characters should have 1 Attack and 1 Wound, with a 5-8+ save.

Characters with top level fighting ability may have 2 Attacks.

Characters with top level defense may have 5+ or even 4+ Saves.

Examples are: 
```
Guard Commander
M6" WS3+ BS3+ A1 W1 Ld6 Sv9+ N1 Pt3
Weapons Melee Melee User 10+/10+
```

```
Space Marine Captain
M6" WS2+ BS 2+ A1 W 1 Ld7 Sv5+ N1 Pt 5
Weapons Melee Melee User 6+ / 6+
Rites of Battle
```

### Heavy Characters
Heavy Characters should generally have 2A 2W

### Vehicles
Vehicles should have 2 weapons, move min 10", and have at least 2W.

They should be bad at WS in general (WS6+)


Light Vehicle
```
Devil Dog
M12" WS6+ BS4+ A1 W2 Ld5 Sv7+ N1 Pt5
Weapons Melee Melee User 10+ / 10+ | Heavy 36" 1 6+/9+ | Heavy 24" 2 10+/4+
```

Heavy Vehicle
```
Leman Russ
M10" WS6+ BS4+ A1 W3 Ld5 Sv6+ N1 Pt11
Weapons Melee Melee User 10+ / 10+ | Heavy 36" 1 6+/9+ | Heavy 72" A1 6+/6+
Grinding Advance: If moving less than 1/2 or remained stationary, double attacks of main cannon.
```

Transport
```
Rhino
M12" WS6+ BS3+ A1 W2 Ld6 Sv6+ N1 Pt5
Weapons Melee Melee User 10+/10+ | Small Arms 24" 1 9+/10+ Rapid Fire
```

### Walkers
Walkers should be man speed, but good at everything

They should in general have 2-3W, or 4-5 for titanic walkers.

```
Space Marines - Dreadnought
M5" WS3+ BS3+ A2 W2 Ld6 Sv6+ N1 Pt9
Weapons Melee Melee User 6+/6+ | Heavy 24" 2 6+/8+ | Small Arms 24" 1 9+/10+ Rapid Fire
```

Light Walkers should be general gun platforms
```
Imperial Guard - Sentinel
M9" WS4+ BS4+ A1 W1 Ld5 Sv8+ N1 Pt4
Weapons Melee Melee User 11+/12+ | Heavy 48" 2 7+/8+ 
Infiltrator
```

## Stats and Weapon Points

The following are ballpark costs. Certain costs will go up or down, based on the base unit.

+1W 2pts
BS+1, WS+1, Ld+1 0.5Pts
+1A 1pt
Spd6" => 12" 1pt
Rapid Fire 1pt
Sv10+ => Sv8+ 0.5pt
Sv10+ => Sv6+ 1pt

## Factions

Factions should in general have a main theme.
